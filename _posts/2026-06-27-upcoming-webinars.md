There are two webinars coming up this week if you're interested in solar and
demand response.
- **Consumer Protection Webinar**: Solar United Neighbors is hosting an event on Monday, June 29th at 4pm to share about their work on consumer awareness and how you can take advantage of their vendor-neutral, unbiased advice whether you already have solar or are thinking about going solar.
  - Register [here](https://www.mobilize.us/solarunitedneighbors/event/962768/?emci=7d1ae035-ac5a-f111-8fcb-000d3a18905c&emdi=bd86ee47-b461-f111-8fcb-000d3a14b640&ceid=3980630)
- **ConnectedSolutions Webinars**: Join Green Energy Consumers Alliance's upcoming webinars to learn how National Grid and Eversource demand response programs reward homeowners and small businesses for reducing electricity demand during the hottest summer days. There are two different webinars, one with a focus on battery systems and one with a focus on smart thermostats.

  - Battery Storage & ConnectedSolutions: [July 1st at 7 PM](https://us.list-manage.com/OWDTxoHxhuF?e=35517f4ac5&c2id=39ee1bb7c3a83b78c444b475642e39ab) and [July 10th at noon](https://us.list-manage.com/8JACLBaSEdz?e=35517f4ac5&c2id=39ee1bb7c3a83b78c444b475642e39ab)
