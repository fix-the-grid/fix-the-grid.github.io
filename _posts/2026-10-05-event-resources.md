## Fix The Grid Event!

Join Fix the Grid for a **Demand Reduction Summit** on **November 19, 2026 (11
AM–4 PM)** at Manchester Community College, NH, bringing together stakeholders
from ISO-NE leadership to residential ratepayers to address the barriers
preventing demand reduction from being scaled across New England. With regional
peak demand projections carrying an $8 billion cost differential, the summit
will focus on identifying what information, regulation, and investment are
needed to meaningfully incorporate active demand reduction into grid modeling,
forecasting, and planning at both the regional and distribution levels.
Attendees will leave with a shared understanding of existing barriers, a
documented roadmap to unlock demand reduction's cost-saving and
emissions-reducing potential, and concrete action items assigned by stakeholder,
with a 6-month follow-up to track progress.
