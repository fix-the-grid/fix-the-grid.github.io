- **MA DPU siting and permitting webinar**: The Department of Public Utilities (DPU) invites you to join a Siting & Permitting 101: Large Clean Energy Infrastructure Projects webinar on **September 17, 2026, from 12:00 PM to 1:30 PM**. This virtual webinar is intended for municipal officials, regional planning agencies and community-based organizations interested in learning about the Energy Facilities Siting Board’s (EFSB) new consolidated permit process for large clean energy infrastructure projects. Advanced registration required: [link](https://us06web.zoom.us/webinar/register/WN_JO_txwQmTQuFq4xxViDdXA).
- **ME community choice aggregation series**: With the passage of [LD2112](https://legislature.maine.gov/backend/App/services/getDocument.aspx?documentId=124144), [Community Choice Aggregation](https://www.nationalccea.org/maine) (CCA) has come to Maine! Now, Maine municipalities, individually or together, can buy electricity on behalf of their residential and small commercial electricity customers. The Maine Community Power Alliance is hosting a 3-part online webinar series with [Paul Fenn (Local Power LLC)](https://www.localpower.com/WhoWeAre.html) to educate everyone about the power of CCAs: **Tuesdays Sept 15th, 22nd, and 29th all 12-1pm ET**. To receive the zoom links to attend, please register [here](https://us02web.zoom.us/meeting/register/WInBlPeLQLC0zBHLpnKmwQ#/registration). (Participants who complete all 3 webinars will receive a training certificate from Local Power LLC.)
- **The Resilience and Equity in the Clean Energy Sector Summit** (RECESS): **September 27-29, 2026**. RECESS is a national clean energy conference, nonprofit, and movement advancing an equitable, regenerative clean energy economy. Founded in Detroit, RECESS intentionally invites Black, Indigenous, Brown, and other historically underrepresented communities into meaningful participation, understanding, and awareness of the clean energy ecosystem. By bringing together community members, students, entrepreneurs, researchers, policymakers, and industry leaders, RECESS helps ensure the clean energy transition is inclusive and delivers benefits to the people most impacted by climate change, economic exclusion, and energy injustice. In Detroit, MI. [Register here](https://www.therecessconference.com/register26).
- **Energy Utility Basics Course**: **October 5-9, 2026**. Energy Utility Basics is an intensive course on the fundamental concepts critical to being conversant in today’s energy industry. Course content is updated yearly as technology, regulation and markets evolve. Participants will receive an overview of the history of the electric and natural gas industries, insights into regulatory decision-making, analysis of the current issues facing the energy industry, and more. Attendees will obtain practical knowledge of the operations and technology of the electricity and natural gas industries from extraction, whether from the ground or from renewables, to the customer’s bill. Non-Member pricing: 1,650/1,750 (Early Bird ends 08.28.2026/Regular 08.29.2026 – 9.30.2026). [Register here](<https://wpui.wisc.edu/programs/2026-programs/energy-utility-basics-2026/?utm_source=NARUC+Alerts+%28MM%29&utm_campaign=e3c3757c15-wpui-rto-fundamentals-rto-advanced-25_COPY_01&utm_medium=email&utm_term=0_-2866ef5876-444271891&ct=t(wpui-rto-fundamentals-rto-advanced-25_COPY_01)&mc_cid=e3c3757c15&mc_eid=61cf5cbccd>).
